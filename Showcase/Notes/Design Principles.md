> Good design is as little design as possible.
> — Dieter Rams

## 1. Depth over decoration
Layers should explain hierarchy. A floating menu sits *above* the page, so it blurs what is behind it.

## 2. Light passes through
Glass is never fully opaque. A hint of the content beneath keeps you oriented.

## 3. Motion with purpose
A button that becomes a menu should **grow out of the button**, not appear from nowhere.

> [!note] Rule of thumb
> If an effect does not help you understand where you are, remove it.
